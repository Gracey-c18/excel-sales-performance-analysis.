# Business Recommendations: Sales Performance Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

This project analyses a fictional sales dataset containing 300 transactions from 2025. Microsoft Excel is used to calculate key performance indicators, analyse sales performance, and identify opportunities for improvement.

## 2. Business Problem

Management needs a clearer understanding of sales performance across regions, product categories, and sales channels to support better business decisions.

## 3. Business Objectives

The objectives of this analysis are to:

* Evaluate overall revenue and profitability.
* Compare performance across geographical regions.
* Identify the product categories contributing to sales.
* Compare performance across sales channels.
* Identify opportunities to improve profitability.

## 4. Key Performance Indicators

The analysis uses the following KPIs:

| KPI                   | Purpose                                                      |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Total Revenue         | Measures total sales generated.                              |
| Total Profit          | Measures revenue remaining after the recorded product costs. |
| Number of Orders      | Measures the number of transactions.                         |
| Average Order Revenue | Measures average revenue per transaction.                    |
| Profit Margin         | Measures profit as a percentage of revenue.                  |

## 5. Analysis Approach

Microsoft Excel is used to analyse the dataset through:

* Data tables and calculated columns.
* Excel formulas.
* PivotTables.
* Charts and visual summaries.
* KPI reporting.

The analysis compares revenue and profit across regions, product categories, and sales channels.

## 6. Recommendations

The following are recommendations to investigate after validating the analysis results.

### Recommendation 1: Investigate regional performance

Compare revenue and profit across all regions. Identify high-performing regions and investigate whether their successful practices can be replicated elsewhere.

### Recommendation 2: Review product profitability

Compare revenue, total profit, and profit margin by product. Investigate products with relatively low margins before making pricing or promotional decisions.

### Recommendation 3: Evaluate sales channels

Compare the performance of online, retail, and corporate sales. Assess both revenue and profit to determine which channels contribute the greatest financial value.

### Recommendation 4: Monitor performance regularly

Use a recurring Excel dashboard to track revenue, profit, order volumes, and profit margins. Regular monitoring can help management identify changes and investigate potential issues.

## 7. Limitations

This project uses fictional data created for portfolio practice. The analysis does not represent the performance of a real business.

The dataset does not include customer acquisition costs, operating expenses, returns, or customer satisfaction information. These factors would be useful for a more complete business assessment.

## 8. Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Microsoft Excel can support business performance analysis through formulas, PivotTables, KPIs, and charts.

The next stage is to validate the findings, quantify the differences between business segments, and use the results to develop evidence-based recommendations.
