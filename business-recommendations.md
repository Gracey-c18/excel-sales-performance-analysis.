## 10. Data Quality and Validation

### Objective

Check the reliability and consistency of the sales dataset before drawing business conclusions.

### Validation Checks

| Check                 | Method                 | Purpose                                             |
| --------------------- | ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Duplicate Order IDs   | Conditional Formatting | Identify potentially duplicated transactions.       |
| Missing Values        | COUNTBLANK             | Identify incomplete records.                        |
| Profit Reconciliation | IF and ABS             | Verify that profit equals revenue minus total cost. |

### Business Importance

Data quality checks help reduce the risk of inaccurate reporting and unreliable business recommendations.

### Outcome

Validation results should be reviewed before the dataset is used for further analysis. Any exceptions should be investigated and resolved before reporting final findings.
