## 9. Product Profitability Analysis

### Objective

Evaluate revenue and profit across product categories to identify which categories contribute most to financial performance.

### Method

A PivotTable in Microsoft Excel was created using Category, Sum of Revenue, and Sum of Profit.

### Business Questions

* Which category generates the highest profit?
* Does the highest-revenue category also generate the highest profit?
* Which categories may require further investigation into pricing or costs?

### Business Implications

Comparing revenue with profit helps management distinguish between sales volume and profitability. This can support better decisions about product pricing, promotional activities, and resource allocation.

### Next Steps

Document the highest-performing category, compare profit margins, and investigate the factors that may explain differences in performance.
